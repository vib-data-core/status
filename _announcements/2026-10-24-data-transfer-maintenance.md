Globus will be unavailable for up to one hour on Saturday 24 October, between 16:30 and 17:30, for planned maintenance.

During this window you will not be able to start new Globus transfers or manage existing ones. Please plan large or time-critical transfers outside this window. More information is available on the [official Globus website](https://www.globus.org/blog/2026-10-24-maintenance).
