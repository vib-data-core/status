Globus will be unavailable for up to one hour on Saturday 24 October, between 16:30 and 17:30, for planned maintenance.

During this window you will not be able to start new Globus transfers or manage existing ones. Transfers that are already running may be paused and should resume once the service is back, but please plan large or time-critical transfers outside this window.
